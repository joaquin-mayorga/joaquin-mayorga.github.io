[Just and Zilberman (1983)](https://www.jstor.org/stable/2662653), *Stochastic Structure, Farm Size and Technology Adoption in Developing Agriculture*
- Farmers choose the share of land under old and new agricultural technology. Output per unit of land depends on the mean and variance the old and new technologies. The optimal allocation of land to old and new technologies depends on interactions between relative risk aversion and variance-covariance of new and old technologies. 

[Herrendorf, Rogerson, and Valentinyi (2014)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/chapter/handbook/pii/B9780444535405000069), *Growth and Structural Transformation*<br>
- Structural transformation is "the reallocation of economic activity across the broad sectors agriculture, manufacturing, and services."
- Three measures to measure structural transformation: employment shares, value added shares, and final consumption expenditure shares.
