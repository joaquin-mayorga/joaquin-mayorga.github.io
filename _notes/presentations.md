


- Fertility rates are falling everywhere.
- You see women no longer having 3 children, some women going from two to one, and some going from one to zero. Suddenly, the fertility rate is below replacement. 
- Income increase explains why in Sub-Saharan Africa.
- A commonly-heard hypothesis is that smartphone adoption drives the fertility decline. That's disproved by Italy's flat fertility rate since the mid 1990s, before smartphones. Socialist countries from Eastern Europe also showed drops in fertility unaccounted for phones or income increases. 
- In the rest of the world nowadays, the culprit is modernity: the organization of life around credentialism and large bureaucracies, both public and private. You need a degree in accounting to become an accountant in large organizations. There is no other way to administer large organizations. As modernity spreads, fertility declines. Raising a kid is expensive and requires building human capital in them at the same time that parents are still building it themselves.
