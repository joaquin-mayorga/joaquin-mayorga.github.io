### Herbert S. Klein, *Historia mínima de Bolivia* 
- Recuentos con potencial de novedad: arte, criollos y economía, la Guerra del Chaco. 

### David Reich, *Who We Are and How We Got Here* 
- How to identify "genetic distance"
- Amerindians and population Y
- I won't discuss here two interesting chapters: on India and on the Yamnaya.

### Wassilly Kandinsky, *Concerning the Spiritual in Art* 
- A minimal theory of art.
